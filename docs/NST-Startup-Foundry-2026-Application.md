## One-line description of your startup

An AI-powered proactive preparation engine that takes a user's brain dump — goals, deadlines, habits, documents — and automatically builds optimized, psychologically-informed schedules that adapt in real time, so students and professionals can focus on execution instead of planning.

---

## What problem are you solving? And for whom?

**The Problem:** 86% of college students struggle with time management, 82% of professionals lack any time management system, and the average worker is productive for just 2 hours 53 minutes per day (the rest lost to planning overhead, context-switching, and reorganizing). This isn't a motivation problem — it's a systems problem. People waste massive mental energy on meta-work — deciding what to do next, figuring out when to study, preparing for meetings, reorganizing priorities after disruptions. This "decision fatigue loop" leads to procrastination (80-95% of students), burnout (80%+ of professionals at risk), and guilt spirals. People with ADHD, packed schedules, or multiple competing goals are hit hardest.

Existing tools make this worse: Google Calendar requires manual planning. Notion is a blank canvas that demands constant upkeep. Todoist tracks tasks but doesn't schedule them. None of these adapt when plans inevitably break.

**For whom:** Students juggling coursework, exams, and side projects. Entrepreneurs managing investor meetings, product deadlines, and learning goals simultaneously. Busy professionals who need to stay prepared for meetings, upskill on the side, and maintain healthy habits — all without the mental overhead of constantly replanning.

**What Jarvis does differently:** You brain-dump everything — "I have a physics exam on April 10, I want to learn system design, I don't work well before 11 AM" — and Jarvis handles the rest. It decomposes goals into micro-tasks, respects your energy patterns and habits, builds a mathematically optimal schedule using constraint programming, links relevant study materials from your own documents, and automatically recalibrates when things change. Failure isn't punished — it's treated as data for recalibration. The result: users focus purely on execution, not planning.

## Why does your target segment need YOUR solution if similar solutions already exist?

**Existing tools solve scheduling OR learning — never both.** Reclaim.ai and Motion optimize calendars but have zero understanding of what you're learning, how prepared you are, or what materials you need. NotebookLM understands documents but can't schedule your day. No product connects "what you need to know" with "when you should work on it" and "here are the materials to use."

**Jarvis's unique value stack:**

1. **Brain dump to schedule in seconds** — One natural language input ("I have a physics exam April 10, I learn best after lunch, I want to read 30 mins daily") becomes a fully optimized, conflict-free schedule with micro-tasks, habit blocks, and study material links. No manual planning.

2. **Psychologically-informed, not just mathematically optimal** — We embed WOOP implementation intentions, Temporal Motivation Theory for deadline urgency, and spaced repetition into every schedule. Tasks aren't just time-slotted — they're designed to maximize completion probability based on behavioral science.

3. **Anti-guilt architecture** — When users miss tasks, Jarvis recalibrates instead of showing red "overdue" badges. This is a deliberate design choice backed by research showing shame-based productivity tools increase abandonment rates. Jarvis says "Recalibrating your plan" instead of "You failed."

4. **Local-first AI, privacy by default** — All core intelligence runs on-device (Apple Silicon). Your goals, habits, documents, and schedules never leave your machine unless you explicitly opt for cloud features. No existing competitor offers this.

5. **Document intelligence meets scheduling** — Upload a PDF syllabus, and Jarvis automatically extracts topics, deadlines, and action items, then links them to relevant tasks in your schedule. This bridges the gap between "knowing what to study" and "having time to study it."

We're not a better calendar or a better to-do list. We're the first proactive preparation engine — an AI that keeps everything you need ready at the right time, so you never have to think about planning again.

## Describe your Idea in Detail — how technology/AI brings true value add

**Is Jarvis a vitamin or a painkiller?**

Jarvis is a painkiller. The problem it solves — decision fatigue, planning overhead, schedule collapse after disruptions — cannot be solved without AI. A human cannot optimally decompose a goal into 25-minute micro-tasks, cross-reference it against existing habits, uploaded documents, and deadlines, then produce a conflict-free schedule in seconds. This requires constraint programming (OR-Tools CP-SAT), natural language understanding (LLMs), and document intelligence (Docling + vector embeddings) working together. No amount of manual effort replicates this — which is exactly why 82% of people have no time management system. The existing solutions require too much human effort to maintain.

**Our working prototype — tech stack and how it was built:**

The Jarvis Engine is a fully functional backend with 57+ implemented components, built over 3 months:

- **AI Layer:** LiteLLM Hybrid Router routes between local Qwen-27B/4B models (on Apple Silicon M4 Pro) and Gemini cloud API. A 4B SLM classifier extracts 8 structured fields from a single brain dump — goals, habits, deadlines, state updates, action items, search queries, calendar text, and knowledge flags — in one prompt call.

- **Scheduling Engine:** Google OR-Tools CP-SAT constraint solver produces mathematically optimal schedules. It handles hard/soft time blocks, task dependencies, habit slots, adaptive daily caps (90–240 min based on cognitive load), and Temporal Motivation Theory priority weighting. When a schedule is infeasible, it automatically retries with expanded horizons (48h → 72h → 5d → 10d → 20d → 30d) — this is our anti-guilt architecture in action.

- **Document Intelligence:** IBM Docling extracts structured content from PDFs. Chunks are embedded via MLX-Embed and stored in ChromaDB. A cosine similarity linker (threshold >= 0.65) automatically connects document content to matching tasks — so when you upload a syllabus, your schedule already knows what materials to use for each study session.

- **Psychological Science:** Every schedule embeds WOOP implementation intentions (mental contrasting + if-then planning), Construal Level Theory (abstract goals → concrete actions), SM-2 spaced repetition for habits, and anti-guilt recalibration instead of shame-based "overdue" labels.

- **Frontend:** Next.js 14 demo with SSE streaming, real-time thinking process display, FullCalendar schedule views, task workspace with RAG-powered study materials, and document management.

**Does it work better than what exists?**

Yes — because nothing else combines all three capabilities. Motion and Reclaim.ai do scheduling but have zero document understanding or learning optimization. NotebookLM does document Q&A but can't schedule. Jarvis is the first to connect "what you need to know" with "when to work on it" and "here are the materials" — all from a single natural language input, with psychologically-informed adaptation that existing tools don't attempt.
